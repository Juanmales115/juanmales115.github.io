Probabilistic Deep Learning

Gaussian VAE with Full Covariance via Cholesky Factorization

Code & Derivations

Standard Variational Autoencoders enforce a mean-field assumption where the approximate posterior covariance is constrained to be diagonal ($\Sigma_\theta(x) = \text{diag}(\sigma_1^2, \dots, \sigma_d^2)$), discarding pairwise correlations between latent dimensions. In this PyTorch project, I parameterized a full covariance posterior matrix $\Sigma_\theta(x) \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times d}$ while guaranteeing positive definiteness through its lower-triangular Cholesky factor $L(x)$:

Analytical Formulation: $$\Sigma_\theta(x) = L(x) L(x)^T, \quad \text{with } L_{ii} = \exp(l_{ii}) > 0 \quad \text{and reparameterization } z = \mu_\theta(x) + L(x)\epsilon, \; \epsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I_d)$$ The encoder network predicts the mean $\mu_\theta(x) \in \mathbb{R}^d$ and $\frac{d(d+1)}{2}$ lower-triangular entries with exponential diagonal stabilization. This enables closed-form analytical computation of the Kullback-Leibler divergence against standard Gaussian priors without numerical matrix inversion instability.

Quantum Information & QML

Variational Quantum Classifier (VQC) with Hardware-Efficient Ansatz

Code & Qiskit Notebook

Developed as part of the selected "Proyecto Ambassador: Computación Cuántica" using Qiskit on the IRIS benchmark dataset. Features are mapped onto the Bloch sphere using an amplitude/rotation encoding with single-qubit $R_X$ and $R_Y$ gates scaled in $[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}]$, coupled with PCA preprocessing to match qubit budget constraints and mitigate quantum decoherence noise.

Explicit Circuit Design & Multi-Class Architecture:
  • Feature Encoding: Single-qubit rotations per feature ($R_X(x_i \cdot \frac{\pi}{2})$ and $R_Y(x_i \cdot \frac{\pi}{2})$) mapping classical inputs into quantum state amplitudes.
  • Hardware-Efficient Ansatz: Parameterized single-layer EfficientSU2 ($R_X$ rotations, reps=1) with linear CNOT entanglement to preserve minimal circuit depth.
  • One-vs-Rest (OvR) Decomposition: Solved Qiskit VQC multiclass limitations by decoupling the 3-species classification into independent binary quantum models trained via COBYLA and SPSA optimizers.
